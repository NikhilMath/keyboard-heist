You’re the IT Support Manager, and life has gone flat. Nothing is especially wrong. Nothing is especially interesting.

Then the CEO sends a ticket. His printer needs a driver. You hate printer drivers.

While he steps out to take a call, you rest your hands on his keyboard. It’s a nothing keyboard, the kind companies buy in bulk. You’ve typed on keyboards for twenty-two years and never noticed one. You notice this one.

You buy the exact same model. It feels like nothing. It has to be his.

Keyboard Heist is a pick-your-path comedy where you make every choice. Case his office, or just ask him for it. Hire a fake grandmother. Stray from the plan and you might fall in love, wind up at orientation in Hell, or stow away on a moon rocket with your mom.

64 sections. 14 endings. A drawing in every section.

It’s just a keyboard. You want it anyway.
