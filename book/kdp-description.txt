Steal a keyboard just to feel something.

You're the IT Support Manager, and life has gone flat. You wake up, fix problems, go home, and wait until it's reasonable to go to bed. Nothing is especially wrong. Nothing is especially interesting.

Then the CEO sends a ticket. His printer needs a driver. You hate printer drivers.

While he steps out to take a call, you rest your hands on his keyboard. It's a nothing keyboard, the kind companies buy in bulk. You've typed on keyboards for twenty-two years and never noticed one. You notice this one.

You buy the exact same model. It feels like nothing. It has to be his.

Now you have a decoy, a spreadsheet and a deadline: the company retreat is next Friday, and he's bringing it. It's the most alive you've felt in months. It's also a crime.

Keyboard Heist is a pick-your-path comedy where you make every choice. Case his office, or just ask him for it. Take down the building's internet to get two minutes alone with it. Hire a fake grandmother. Stray from the plan and you might fall in love, wind up at orientation in Hell (there are deviled eggs), or stow away on a moon rocket with your mom. And Daniel, the nicest CEO you've ever met, has a secret of his own.
