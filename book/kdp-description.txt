Steal a keyboard just to feel something.

You're the IT Support Manager. For months you've woken up, fixed problems, gone home, and waited until it's reasonable to go to bed. Nothing is especially wrong. Nothing is especially interesting. You've reached the plateau, and the view is mostly emails and tickets.

Then the CEO sends a ticket. His printer needs a driver. You hate printer drivers.

His keyboard is nothing special, the kind companies buy in bulk. But you type your name on it, and for the first time in twenty-two years, you notice a keyboard. So you buy the exact same one. It feels like nothing. It has to be his.

So you plan. A spreadsheet. A decoy. A building-wide internet outage. A hired grandmother. It's the most alive you've felt in months, and it's a crime. Or it will be.

Keyboard Heist is a pick-your-path comedy where you make every choice. Case the office, or just ask him about it. Take it right now, or walk away forever. Some paths end in love. Some end in Hell, with paperwork. One takes you to space, where you break a very expensive toilet. And your boss, who is genuinely, relentlessly nice, may not be who he says he is.

• 64 short sections, and every choice is a tap
• 11 real endings and 3 dead ends to find
• An illustration for every section
• Read it in a lunch break, then go back for the endings you missed

Your boss is genuinely nice. The keyboard is genuinely ordinary. You want it anyway.
