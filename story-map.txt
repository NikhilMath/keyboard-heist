THE TRUNK SCENES (each merges scenes from spine-draft.txt; keep the approved beats)

T1 ARRIVAL
   The approved opening: plateau paragraph, "Then a ticket pops up...," Daniel Hall's
   ticket. Three buttons, exactly as you wrote them.

T2 OFFICE
   The approved office scene (Daniel, granola bar, Post-it, kids' photo, he steps
   out, you type on the keyboard, "Something is wrong with me").
   Three buttons: plan it, grab it now, or walk away.

T3 THE PLAN  [merges: drive home, model number, impostor, plan, recon]
   You can't stop typing on the couch. You look it up: $149.99. You buy one and it
   feels like nothing. It has to be his. Decoy. Spreadsheet. Marcy, the retreat
   calendar, "he started the company on it."

T4 THE OUTAGE  [merges: window, outage]
   The doorway attempt (Marcy looks up, "Cable"). Then you take down the internet
   "for an emergency patch." Laptops like sick pets. Marcy: two minutes, snack,
   break room. You have two minutes. Daniel's packing list: "keyboard" underlined.

T5 THE GRANDMOTHER  [merges: realization, casting]
   You can't steal it at a retreat. Daniel can cancel it; he hates leaving anyone
   out (the intern allergic to the trees at the cabin). You need a sick relative.
   The ad, three candidates, Mrs. Cox.

T6 THE VISIT  [merges: briefing, visit, cancels]
   You tell Daniel; "nobody gets left out." He visits with flowers and a casserole.
   Mrs. Cox knows your middle name. Daniel prays. He cancels the retreat. You pay
   Mrs. Cox $150. The room cost almost as much. Three keyboards' worth for one.

T7 THE AUDIT  [merges: audit, label maker, auditor, tour]
   Asset audit email. Your decoy has no tag. The supply closet, the night cleaner.
   The stern auditor arrives, likes you, works toward Daniel's office. You get
   there first.

T8 THE SWAP  [merges: swap, scan]
   The decoy goes on the desk, the real one on the chair. The auditor scans the
   forged tag: green. Then he starts to wheeze.

T9 THE CHOICE  [merges: choice, shock, run]
   He drops to one knee. The keyboard is right there. One second. You're shocked
   at yourself. You grab it and run. Something skitters under your shoe: an
   inhaler. "Dang," you say.

T10 THE LIE  [merges: lie, question, guilt]
   You slide back in and press the inhaler into his hand. "I saw you drop it ten
   seconds ago." He asks why you grabbed the keyboard. "I don't know. I was too
   busy thinking about saving your life." He's moved. By evening everyone knows
   about the inhaler and nobody knows about the keyboard, in your bag. You
   rationalize. Daniel texts: "Heard what you did today. Thank you."

THE 10 ENDINGS (each one short scene; every one ends with the replay button)

E1  HAVE YOU TRIED RESTARTING?  [your original opening choice]
    Daniel: "Oh! Good idea!" He restarts it. It works. "Appreciate you!" Ticket
    closed in two minutes. You never go to his office. You have never felt so
    nothing. Tomorrow there will be a ticket.

E2  CHECK THE PRINTER MODEL FIRST  [your original opening choice]
    You find the driver and install it remotely. Ticket closed at 4:52. You are
    good at your job. It is not enough. You never meet the keyboard.

E3  THE SPARE  [written before, commit 5e5552f]
    You walk out with it. Daniel sees. "It's not working." He shows you the fix
    (unplug the receiver). It works. "Keep it! I have a spare." He hands it to
    you and drops it. It breaks. "Oh. Never mind. I'll use the spare."
    You install the printer driver and go back to your desk.

E4  WALK AWAY  [your open question: end or loop]
    You finish the driver and go home. You do not think about it. Next morning
    your fingers are typing on the steering wheel. Nothing is especially wrong.
    Nothing is especially interesting. Tomorrow there will be a ticket.

E5  THE GIFT
    You ask Daniel about the keyboard. He lights up: "Oh, you like it? Take a
    new one from the supply closet!" It's still in the box. You type your name.
    It's a keyboard. Buying it never worked, and neither does being given one.

E6  CALL YOUR MOTHER
    You tell Daniel your mother in Ohio is very sick. He sends flowers. He calls
    her to pray. She is fine. She is also confused. She is coming for a long
    visit. The keyboard is still on his desk.

E7  METHOD
    You let Mrs. Cox keep going. She is so good that Daniel adopts her: she moves
    into his guest room, comes to the audit, and asks you to pay her in hugs.
    Nobody else knows. You never get near the keyboard again.

E8  ASSET NOT FOUND
    You skip the tag. The auditor scans the decoy. It beeps red. "Asset not
    found." Daniel is heartbroken. "I just wish you'd asked." You are asked to
    leave. The keyboard is still on his desk.

E9  NOT THE HERO
    You keep running. Marcy finds the auditor with a granola bar in her hand.
    He's fine. He tells everyone who ran. You have the keyboard for eleven hours.
    Then HR shows up. It feels like nothing.

E10 THE PLATEAU  [canonical, your ending]
    You're home. The keyboard is on the kitchen table. You type your name. It
    feels like a keyboard. It always did. What you felt in Daniel's office was
    never the keyboard. You backspace. Even the backspace is fine. You got what
    you wanted. Nothing is especially wrong. Nothing is especially interesting.
    Tomorrow there will be a ticket.
