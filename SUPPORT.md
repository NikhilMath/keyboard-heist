# Support

## Common questions

**My progress disappeared.**
Progress is saved in your browser on your device. It won't carry over to another browser or device, and it's erased if you clear your browser data or use a private window.

**How do I start again without losing what I've found?**
Use **Start over and do NOT clear history**. To wipe everything, use **Start over AND clear history**.

**I hit a dead end.**
Press the button under it to go back to your last choice, or click any dot on **Your map** to jump to a scene you've already reached.

**The site looks out of date.**
Your browser may be showing an old copy. Reload with Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac or Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue at https://github.com/NikhilMath/keyboard-heist/issues and include:

- what you clicked, and what you expected to happen
- what happened instead
- your browser and device (for example, Safari on an iPhone)
