# Code of conduct

Keyboard Heist is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else connected to it) is expected to follow these rules.

## Do

- Be kind and respectful, even when you disagree.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Welcome people of every background, identity and experience level.

## Don't

- Harass, insult or threaten anyone.
- Use sexual, hateful or discriminatory language or images.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.
- Keep doing something after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the owner, Nikhil Math, privately through https://nikhilmath.com. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The owner reviews every report and decides what to do. That can range from a private warning, to removing comments or contributions, to a permanent ban from the project.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
